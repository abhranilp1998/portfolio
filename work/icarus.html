01 / THE CHALLENGE

An independent artist. An identity with room to grow.

Several experiments and external links made it difficult to find the artist, start listening, or choose a meaningful next step.

02 / INSIDE THE IMPLEMENTATION

01

Keep the artist’s world intact

A supplied illustration, original performance photos and source-backed biography anchor the identity.

02

Put listening where visitors expect it

A dedicated room surfaces SoundCloud embeds, playback coordination and clear archive fallbacks.

03

Replace detours with direct choices

An on-site links hub exposes release-specific services; distinct contact routes lead to email drafts.

03 / WHERE IT STANDS

SoundCloud availability and service access are external dependencies. Contact uses the visitor’s email app. Earlier pages remain accessible through a dedicated archive.
