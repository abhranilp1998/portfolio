## Material-generation prompt

Create a production UI texture asset: a perfectly flat, orthographic, seamless tile of fine horizontal brushed satin aluminum, medium pale silver gray, neutral cool tone. 1024 by 1024 square. Very fine subtle anisotropic hairline grain, low contrast, even diffuse lighting, no bright highlights, no reflections, no objects, no lettering, no icons, no borders, no corners, no shadows, no perspective, no dramatic gradient. This will be tiled as the material of tactile software window titlebars and physical-looking buttons on a skeuomorphic developer portfolio. Material-only texture; uniform and understated enough that dark UI text stays readable over it.

## Other sources

The minimize, expand and close SVGs are original Ionicons 5.5.2 assets from its published package (unpkg.com/ionicons@5.5.2/dist/svg/). They are bundled locally and attributed in ICONS-LICENSE.txt. No runtime icon CDN is required.

Profile facts, 19 tools, all career/education entries and four testimonials come from archive/original/index.html. Project data is shared from assets/rehaul/projects.mjs. Blog topics remain explicitly planned and come from assets/content/posts.json. Client names/logos remain placeholders. No real publishing or authentication backend is introduced by the worlds.
