01 / THE CHALLENGE

Tomorrow’s commute, a little less uncertain.

Planning a journey is not just finding a route. It means deciding when to leave, including a buffer, and knowing whether information is real or illustrative.

02 / INSIDE THE IMPLEMENTATION

01

Persistence that can evolve

Drift provides a shared SQLite model, schema migrations and a path from earlier local data.

02

Maps with an honest data boundary

OpenStreetMap powers place selection. Google Directions supplies live routes when configured; otherwise the app labels example Kolkata routes.

03

A plan that survives app restarts

Saved trips, lifecycle refresh and Android background work support the planning flow beyond a single session.

03 / WHERE IT STANDS

Without a configured Maps key, routes are explicitly example data. Android background notifications and browser capabilities differ. The repository’s demo is not a promise of live transit coverage.
